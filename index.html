Magic: The Gathering® Old Frame Format Deck Validator

To copy your deck list from Moxfield:

  1. Open your deck on Moxfield.
  2. Under the More menu, click Export.
  3. Click Copy Plain Text from the export options.
  4. Paste deck contents into the Deck List field below.
